Healthcare · Personal project2026

Outpatient No-Show Risk Predictor

Predicts which outpatient appointments are likely to be missed, so clinics can focus reminders and rebooking where they matter most.

  • XGBoost on 110,516 public appointments, with leakage-safe history features and time-based validation
  • Contacting the top 20% highest-risk appointments reaches 37% of no-shows, about 1.9× random
  • Calibrated scores, fairness check, drift monitoring and 88 automated tests
  • LLM assistant writes staff summaries and patient reminders that never mention risk or history

How I build

01

The model predicts. The LLM explains.

The language model only receives the score and SHAP drivers, and is never allowed to change or invent a number.

02

Guardrails come from testing.

I read real outputs, catch the failure, and fix it with a targeted rule, then test again.

03

Know what the data can't see.

SHAP shows associations, not causes. Every project states its limits, from data drift to missing complaint history.
